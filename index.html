About the Author

A journey still being written.

Kamarudeen Sodiq Alade was born on 9 April 1999 in Oyo State, Nigeria. He grew up in a polygamous family as the second child of his father and was raised alongside many siblings.

His educational journey was not always easy. After secondary school, he studied Environmental Health at the College of Health Technology, Ilesha, completing a three-year programme. He is currently furthering his education in Environmental Health Science at the National Open University of Nigeria.

Kamarudeen is a young writer interested in faith, human relationships, personal growth, conflict, peace, and ideas that encourage people to think more deeply about themselves and the world around them.

My Mission

Make life better, not perfect.

I do not believe the world can ever be perfect. But I believe people can choose to leave it better than they found it. Through writing, I want to encourage thoughtful conversation, personal growth, understanding across differences, and a willingness to learn from one another.

“If the world cannot be perfect, we can still choose to leave it better than we found it.”
